# Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky), round R1

- **Anchor used:** Shklovsky, *Art as Technique*: habit devours things, and the device is there to make the stone stony, to make perception slow and hard. The strangest device has to sit where the eye lands.
- **Observed:** index_html-desk.png shows the library as a model-kit sprue: an orange runner, gates, and numbered pieces A1 to G1 hung on a frame. In index-night-desk.png the frame stays paper and orange while the pieces go dark, and B2 and G1 flip to light. This estranges "component library". Inside the pieces sit the stock forms: C3 is a stat row (412 / 68% / 3%), and C5 is a three-tier pricing table with a "Most chosen" badge. xray-pricing-desk.png is the strongest device on the site. It reduces a pricing table to tagged boxes (article [plan], h3, p $650), so the table becomes visible as made. On the landing it appears only below the fold ("See it bare", y≈784). In sandbox_html_t_lagos-event-desk.png it is an unchecked box labelled "(preview only)".
- **Findings:**
  - F1 [claim] The sprue is a real device. It shows the parts as parts. R0 F1 is answered at the level of the site, though not inside the parts.
  - F2 [defect] The strangest device, X-ray, is buried: it is off by default, below the fold, and smallest in the sandbox. This is the R0 F2/F4 "bare / built" request, built but hidden.
  - F3 [claim] R0 F3 is answered. navbar.html and social-media.html are deleted, a grep of parts/, site/ and templates/ finds no "Twitter" strings, and the footer marks are labelled as generic.
  - F4 [premise] The stat tiles and the SaaS pricing rows are habit, but they are what the brief catalogues. The sprue frames them as specimens, not as the new. I accept this.
- **Verdict:** OBJECT (narrow)
- **Requested change:** F2: put a bare/built switch on the sprue itself, on the first screen, next to "Theme every part", so that all 18 pieces can be seen X-rayed at once. F1, F3, F4: none.
